# Product Vision & Positioning: LeadPulse

> **Product Name**: **LeadPulse**  
> **Tagline**: The Autonomous Inbound Sentinel & Instant Rescue Engine for Service Trades  
> **Target Customer**: SMB Home Service & Trade Contractors (1–25 technicians in US, UK, AU, CA)  

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Every day, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and roofing contractors lose \$25,000–\$45,000+ per month in high-intent inbound customer revenue because:
1. Inbound phone calls go unanswered while staff is in the field or on another line.
2. Web forms and quote requests sit in email inboxes for hours or days.
3. After-hours and weekend inquiries become dead leads by the next morning.
4. Callers refuse to leave voicemail and immediately hire the first competing contractor who responds.

---

## 2. Value Proposition & Unfair Advantage
LeadPulse acts as an **autonomous, 24/7 front-desk sentinel** that plugs directly into a contractor's phone lines, web forms, and lead sources. 

Within **under 10 seconds** of a missed call or new inquiry, LeadPulse:
- Instantly triages urgency and estimates job value using lightweight NLP.
- Fires an autonomous, personalized interactive SMS/WhatsApp auto-rescue sequence.
- Enables the customer to confirm their emergency, share photos, or book an open inspection slot instantly before calling a competitor.
- Surfaces a real-time **Mission Control Triage Radar** with countdown SLA clocks and a live **Financial Revenue Rescued Ledger**.

---

## 3. Core Success Metrics (North Star)
1. **Speed-to-First-Rescue**: $< 10\text{ seconds}$ from inbound event to customer acknowledgment.
2. **Leaked Lead Recovery Rate**: $> 45\%$ of otherwise lost missed calls converted into qualified conversations or bookings.
3. **Operator Triage Latency**: $< 30\text{ seconds}$ for a human dispatcher to reassign or accept an active high-urgency lead.
4. **Quantifiable ROI**: Clear demonstration of \$10,000+ in saved pipeline revenue within the first 30 days of operation.
