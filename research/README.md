# Research Artifacts & Evidence Repository

## Purpose
This directory stores all empirical research, market analysis, competitor breakdowns, user interviews/surveys, and domain intelligence produced by the **Researcher**, **Market/Competitor Analyst**, and **Customer/ICP Analyst**.

---

## Directory Structure
- `01_market_overview.md`: Market size, dynamics, and macroeconomic vectors.
- `02_competitor_matrix.md`: Direct/indirect competitors, features, pricing, strengths, weaknesses.
- `03_icp_and_personas.md`: Ideal customer profiles, buyer persona cards, jobs-to-be-done (JTBD).
- `04_lead_leakage_taxonomy.md`: Exhaustive taxonomy of leak mechanisms across the revenue funnel.
- `evidence/`: Raw data, benchmark summaries, quoted research studies, and citations.

---

## Rules of Engagement
1. **Observable Evidence Only**: No hallucinated facts or unverified statistics. All claims must link to primary sources or validated benchmarks.
2. **Artifact Delivery**: All research outputs must be committed as markdown files with actionable takeaway summaries.
