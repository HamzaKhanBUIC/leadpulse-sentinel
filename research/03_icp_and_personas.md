# Vertical Evaluation, ICP & Persona Dossiers

## 1. Vertical Comparison & Wedge Selection Analysis

To select the strongest opportunity within the lead leakage problem space, we evaluated the 4 candidate verticals across 5 critical dimensions:

| Dimension | Home Services & Trades (HVAC, Plumbing, Roofing) | Aesthetic / Dental Clinics | Digital & Creative Agencies | Management / Tech Consultants |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Urgency / Speed Sensitivity** | **Extreme** (Caller switches to competitor in <5 min) | **High** (Patient books first open clinic) | **Medium** (Prospect expects reply within 24h) | **Low** (RFP / consultative cycle) |
| **Missed Call / Lead Leak Rate** | **Very High (25%–62%)** | **Moderate (20%–30%)** | **Low-Medium (10%–20%)** | **Low (5%–15%)** |
| **Financial Loss per Leaked Lead** | **High** (\$400 repair to \$15,000 replacement) | **Medium-High** (\$200 to \$3,000 treatment) | **Very High** (\$5,000–\$50,000 retainer) | **Very High** (\$10,000–\$100,000 engagement) |
| **Lead Volume per Month** | **High (100–500 inquiries/mo)** | **High (150–400 inquiries/mo)** | **Low (10–50 inquiries/mo)** | **Very Low (5–20 inquiries/mo)** |
| **Regulatory / Compliance Overhead** | **Zero / Low** (Standard consumer privacy) | **Extreme** (HIPAA / GDPR / Medical records) | **Low** | **Low** |
| **Wedge Attractiveness Score** | **9.5 / 10 (WINNER)** | **6.5 / 10** | **6.0 / 10** | **4.5 / 10** |

### Wedge Decision Rationale
**Home Services & Specialized Trade Contractors** in the US, UK, Australia, and Canada represent the strongest initial wedge because:
1. **The Leakage is Real-Time & Lethal**: When a homeowner with a burst pipe or broken heater gets voicemail, they hang up and call the next Google listing in under 30 seconds.
2. **Clear Operational Bottleneck**: Technicians and business owners are on roofs, under sinks, or driving vans; they physically cannot answer the phone or reply to web forms while working.
3. **Instant Quantifiable ROI**: Rescuing just 1–2 leaked leads per month delivers a 10x–20x return on software investment.
4. **No Heavy Compliance Blocks**: Unlike healthcare/clinics, home services can be captured, triaged, and booked with zero HIPAA/medical liability risks.

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Firmographics
- **Target Geography**: United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada (English-speaking service markets).
- **Sub-Industries**: HVAC, Plumbing, Roofing, Electrical, Emergency Water/Fire Restoration, Garage Door Repair.
- **Company Size**: 2 to 25 employees (1–15 service vans / field technicians).
- **Monthly Revenue**: \$30,000 to \$350,000 / month.
- **Current Inbound Channels**: Google Local Services Ads (LSA), Google Business Profile phone calls, Website contact forms, Emergency Yelp/Angi webhooks.
- **Current Tech Stack**: Basic phone line / VoIP (RingCentral, Google Voice, Grasshopper), basic CRM or spreadsheet (Jobber, Housecall Pro, or plain Gmail).

## 3. Buyer & User Persona Cards

### Persona 1: "Dave the Owner-Operator" (Economic Buyer)
- **Role**: Founder & Master Technician
- **Demographics**: Age 38–55, runs an 8-person HVAC & Plumbing shop.
- **Daily Reality**: Spends 60% of his day in the field supervising complex jobs or quoting bids. Phone rings constantly while he is holding tools.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Pays \$3,000/mo on Google Ads, but knows half the calls ring through to voicemail when the office coordinator is on lunch or on another call.
  - Frustrated when he checks voicemail at 6:00 PM and finds 5 homeowners who already booked someone else at 11:00 AM.
- **Core JTBD**: *"Stop my marketing dollars from burning and ensure every single inbound lead is instantly captured and booked before my competitors get them."*

### Persona 2: "Sarah the Office Coordinator" (Primary Operator)
- **Role**: Dispatcher & Customer Service Representative
- **Demographics**: Age 25–45, handles phone lines, scheduling, and billing.
- **Daily Reality**: High cognitive load; juggling phone calls, dispatching technicians, ordering parts, and answering emails.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Cannot handle 2 simultaneous phone calls; 2nd caller always hits voicemail.
  - Web forms sit in an email inbox for hours before she has time to type a reply.
  - Lacks an instant visibility dashboard showing which leads are about to expire.
- **Core JTBD**: *"Give me a single live screen that automatically catches missed callers via SMS, answers their basic emergency questions, and lets me schedule them in one click."*
